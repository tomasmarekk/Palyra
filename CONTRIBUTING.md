## Developer Certificate of Origin

Contributions must include a `Signed-off-by` trailer certifying the Developer
Certificate of Origin 1.1:

```text
Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
```

Use `git commit --signoff` or `git commit -s` when creating commits.

The sign-off certifies that you wrote the contribution, or otherwise have the
right to submit it under the project license, and that the contribution can be
redistributed under the project license.
