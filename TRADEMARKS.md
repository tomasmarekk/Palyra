You may use Palyra brand identifiers when the use is accurate and not confusing:

- To refer to the original Palyra project.

- To distribute unmodified Palyra with preserved notices.
- To identify that your work is based on or derived from Palyra, as long as your

## Restricted Uses

You may not use Palyra brand identifiers in a way that is likely to confuse users
or misrepresent origin, sponsorship, or endorsement. In particular, you may not:

- Brand a fork, modified version, hosted service, package, binary, extension, or
  commercial offering as Palyra without written permission.
- Use a name, logo, domain, or visual identity that is confusingly similar to
  Palyra for a public-facing fork, product, service, or distribution.
- Remove or obscure Palyra attribution while presenting the original project as
  your own work.
- State or imply that the Palyra project or tomasmarekk endorses your fork,
  product, service, company, or distribution.

Public forks and modified distributions should use their own distinct name,
branding, logos, domains, and product identity.
